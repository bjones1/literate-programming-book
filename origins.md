Classical literate programming
==============================

> The idea is that you do not document programs (after the fact), but write
> documents that contain the programs.
>
> —John Max Skaller,
> [Interviews with Creators of Vyper and Stackless Python](https://gnosis.cx/publish/programming/charming_python_8.html)

1. Knuth, D. E. (1984). [Literate programming](course_materials/knuthweb.pdf).
   The Computer Journal, 27(2), 97–111.
2. [CWEB](https://mirrors.mit.edu/CTAN/web/cweb/cwebman.pdf)
3. Modern tools: [Literate](https://zyedidia.github.io/literate/)
   ([main.lit](https://zyedidia.github.io/literate/literate-source/main.html) /
   [hello world](https://zyedidia.github.io/literate/manual.html#example-program)),
